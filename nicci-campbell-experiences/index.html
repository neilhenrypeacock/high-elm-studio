Weddings and events that feel like the people in them

Since 2014, Nicci Campbell Experiences has been dreaming up, styling and running weddings and events at Ground The Venue, a family farm in the Cradle of Humankind.

Every one starts in the same place: how you want the day to feel. The Pinterest board, the half-formed idea, the thing you saw once and can’t stop thinking about. Then it becomes real.
